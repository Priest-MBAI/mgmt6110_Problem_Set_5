My agent card | Internship MailBot | finished 17 Oct 2026, by 6:37 PM

  1. The job: Tell me, a Master of Business AI student, when an email shows an internship opening, or an update on an internship I applied for.
  2. The trigger: A new email in my personal Gmail that matches the saved search, checked about every 15 minutes.
  3. The tools: Gmail, new email matching a search (trigger); Email by Zapier, Send Outbound Email (action).
  4. It must never: Email anyone but me. The action's recipient is fixed to my own address, and it has no reply or forward step.
  5. Where a person checks: I read every alert and open the original posting before I apply, and I look at Zap history weekly.

Q1 | Where Internship MailBot sits on the map

(a) My quadrant: Agents run it, a person judges.

(b) The two questions. Is there a right answer? Not for the job I need done. The Zap can check whether an email holds my exact words, but whether an internship fits me has no single right answer, and my runs show the gap: the marketing email held my date phrase and fired, though I would never apply, and a made-up email for a role I would want was missed because it was worded differently. Must people and AI work together, or can either do it alone? Together: the Zap watches and alerts me on its own, within about 75 seconds of an email arriving (sent 02:35:58, run 02:37:11), but it cannot judge fit, so the job is only done when I judge each alert.

(c) The person in the loop. I stay at the gate: I read every alert and open the original posting before I apply, and I look at Zap history weekly for misses and repeats, like the extra runs my own forwards caused. The agent may watch my Gmail, match words and email me, nothing else: its recipient is fixed to my own address and it has no reply or forward step. It would move to "AI alone, with checks" if its job were only to flag an exact phrase and nothing depended on fit.

(d) My group's view. I agree with most of my group's placements, such as the sensor alerts and the order-tracking replies, where one right answer exists and a check can confirm it. I would answer the first question differently for the school-mail alert: my own agent showed that matching words fires on the wrong role and misses a good one worded differently, and "does this email concern me?" has the same problem, so it has no single right answer. I would move it to "Agents run it, a person judges", because the agent only surfaces emails and the student judges each one, and a weekly look at Zap history is too slow a check for an inbox that carries deadlines.

Collaboration log

  • What I delegated to AI: the agent card wording, the Gmail search, the page edits, the test emails, and publishing the role-word fix as version 3.
  • What I checked, and how: each alert arrived in my inbox with the right subject (screenshots), each run matches an entry in Zap history, and the rerun after the fix produced no run. The card was committed to the page before the first run.
  • One AI error I caught: the AI told me it could not send the test emails from its side. I asked "can't you do it?", and it found it could.
  • Time and cost: about 3 hours, and no money spent: Gmail and Email by Zapier, on Zapier's trial, with no purchase made.

Reflection

Where it fitted: Internship MailBot fitted the watching part of my internship search: it caught every test email that used the words in its search and alerted me within about 75 seconds, so I no longer have to scan my inbox myself.

Where it failed: It failed wherever reading for meaning was needed, because version 3 fired on a marketing internship I would never apply for and missed a role I wanted only because it said "Intern" rather than "internship".

What I changed: So in version 4 I widened the search to every form of the word, stopped it re-firing on its own alerts, and put each email's subject in the alert, leaving the judgment of fit to me, the student in the loop.
